# C2: Cinematic Photo Prompts

## What It Covers

Film-like, documentary, and realistic photography styles. CCTV footage, vintage film, Polaroid, movie stills, news footage, and candid photography.

## Key Techniques

### 1. Camera/Medium Specification

Specify the camera type or photographic medium:
- Film: "35mm film photograph", "Kodak Portra 400", "Polaroid", "Technicolor"
- Digital: "iPhone selfie", "smartphone photo", "DSLR"
- Surveillance: "CCTV footage", "security camera", "dashcam"
- Style: "Amateur, mediocre iPhone selfie", "professional headshot"

### 2. Film Stock & Grain

For vintage/film look:
- "visible film grain"
- "Frame from a 1953 motion picture in technicolor"
- "Grainy security camera footage"
- "Scanline effects"

### 3. Shot Composition

Describe the shot type:
- "full shot from behind"
- "close-up of"
- "over-the-shoulder shot"
- "drone shot"
- "wide-angle"
- "top-angle"

### 4. Color & Atmosphere

Set the mood through color:
- "muted color palette" — serious, dramatic
- "desaturated" — documentary feel
- "warm golden tones" — nostalgic
- "high contrast" — dramatic
- "natural colors" — realistic

## Prompt Templates

### Film Still
```
[Film stock/format], [shot type] of [subject description]. [Setting details]. [Color palette], [atmosphere/mood], [era if applicable].
```

### Documentary/Surveillance
```
[Camera type] footage, [location/context]. [Subject description]. [Activity/action]. [Visual quality/film characteristics].
```

### Candid/Amateur Photo
```
[Camera type] of [subject]. [Setting]. [Time of day], [weather/lighting]. [Casual/imperfect quality indicators].
```

### Portrait
```
[Portrait style] of [subject]. [Pose/expression]. [Background/setting]. [Lighting quality], [camera/lens detail], [aspect ratio].
```

## Example Prompts

**CCTV/Surveillance:**
```
cctv footage, UK. a man in the street wearing gigantic oversized sneakers. talking to a police officer.
```

**Cinematic Film Still:**
```
35mm film photograph, full shot from behind of an elderly man in a long black overcoat and an elderly woman in a long deep red overcoat, standing symmetrically on a vast concrete tarmac. They are looking at a Northrop Grumman B-2 Spirit stealth bomber parked in the middle distance. In the far background are hazy, snow-capped mountains under a pale blue sky. The top third of the image is framed by the interior ceiling of a massive hangar with horizontal beams. Cinematic and formal composition, muted color palette, visible film grain, somber and powerful atmosphere.
```

**Amateur/Casual:**
```
Amateur, mediocre iPhone selfie of smiling young actress dressed as Anne Boleyn. Outdoor movie set setting, daytime, clear blue sky.
```

**Period Film:**
```
A soviet biology textbook open to the page describing how to hybridize a human and a wolf, photo realistic - being read by a werewolf with a sparkly vampire in a glass cage in the background. 1940's styling. Frame from a 1953 motion picture in technicolor.
```

**Cinematic Portrait:**
```
Over-the-shoulder shot, 90年代, looking at a TV broadcasting a Nano-Banana-Pro commercial. Rain-streaked window, neon reflections on wet glass. Moody film noir lighting, VHS tracking artifacts, 4:3 aspect ratio.
```

## Tips

- "CCTV footage" creates authentic surveillance look with scanlines and timestamps
- "Amateur, mediocre" creates intentionally imperfect photos
- Era + film format creates period-accurate looks
- Shot type (from behind, over-shoulder) adds cinematic composition
- Weather and lighting are powerful mood setters: "rain-streaked", "golden hour", "overcast"
- Adding "VHS tracking artifacts", "scanlines" adds period-specific imperfections
