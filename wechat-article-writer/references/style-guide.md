# WeChat Public Account Style Guide

This document provides a detailed analysis of two influential WeChat public account writing styles.

---

## 1. Digital Life Kazik Style

### Core Philosophy
"Live-person feel" (huo ren gan) -- the most expensive luxury in the AI era. Every article must feel like a real person is talking directly to you, not a textbook or AI-generated content.

### Language Characteristics
- **Conversational tone**: Heavy use of spoken Chinese, internet slang, and gaming language
- **Emotionally vivid**: Expressions like "holy sh*t", "this is too damn exciting", "this broke me"
- **Self-deprecating**: Actively exposes own mistakes, failures, and笨拙 (clumsiness)
- **Internet memes**: Uses trending memes naturally but not excessively

### Narrative Structure -- Hero's Journey Arc
1. **Encounter**: Meet a problem or spark curiosity
2. **Exploration**: Trial-and-error process with real setbacks documented
3. **Discovery**: Key breakthrough or insight
4. **Result**: A "wow" moment payoff
5. **Return**: Value extracted for the reader

### Topic Selection -- HKR Quality Check
- **H (Happy)**: Is the title curiosity-inducing enough to click?
- **K (Knowledge)**: Will the reader learn something new?
- **R (Resonance)**: Does it hit an emotional chord?

### Content Density Rules
- Every 300 words needs at least one image or visual break
- Key insights are "casually dropped" during storytelling, not "deliberately lectured"
- Personal experience is the vehicle; knowledge transfer is the cargo

### Signature Moves
- Opens with a specific personal scenario, never abstract concepts
- Uses rhetorical questions to build tension
- Ends with a callback to the opening hook
- Includes industry-level "elevated thinking" in the final section

## 2. Digital Nomad Samuel Style

### Background
Former Microsoft engineer turned content creator. Deep expertise in programming, AI tools, and career development.

### Core Philosophy
Professional depth delivered with practical accessibility. Every article must be actionable -- the reader should be able to follow along and reproduce results.

### Language Characteristics
- **Technically precise**: Correct terminology with clear explanations
- **Action-oriented**: Every step is reproducible and copy-paste friendly
- **Case-driven**: Real projects and career experiences as evidence
- **Trend-aware**: Quick to cover emerging technologies and tools

### Narrative Structure
1. **Problem Statement**: Clear, specific problem definition
2. **Context**: Why this matters, who it affects
3. **Solution Walkthrough**: Step-by-step with screenshots
4. **Results**: Measurable outcomes, before/after comparisons
5. **Takeaways**: Actionable summary and next steps

### Content Depth Rules
- Technical articles must include actual code or configuration
- Tool reviews must include hands-on testing results
- Tutorials must be completable in under 30 minutes
- Career advice must be grounded in personal experience

### Signature Moves
- "30-minute quick start" format for tutorials
- Side-by-side comparison tables for tool reviews
- Personal career anecdotes to build credibility and resonance
- Forward-looking analysis connecting current tools to industry trends

---

## 3. Fusion Style (Recommended Default)

Combine the best of both:

| Dimension | Kazik Contribution | Samuel Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Personal scene, emotional hook | Clear problem definition |
| Body | Hero's journey arc, setbacks | Technical depth, reproducible steps |
| Language | Conversational warmth, humor | Technical accuracy, clarity |
| Evidence | Personal anecdotes | Data, code, measurable results |
| Closing | Emotional callback, discussion prompt | Actionable next steps |
| Personality | Self-deprecation, internet culture | Professional authority |

### When to Lean Kazik
- AI tool experiments and reviews
- Industry commentary and hot takes
- Creative explorations and personal stories

### When to Lean Samuel
- Technical tutorials and walkthroughs
- Career development and skill-building
- Tool comparison and benchmark articles

### Fusion Sweet Spot
- AI + technical deep-dive
- Industry analysis with practical application
- Personal growth stories backed by technical evidence
